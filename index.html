Getting around

  • Hold any sound for ½ second (a Chopper slice, a Play pad or key, a piano key, a group, a Parts row or stem, a slice-bin tile) and it goes to the next empty pad in Pad Studio. A message shows where it went, with Go to pad and Undo.
  • Hold an empty pad (or any pad in ✋ Arrange or ✏️ Edit) and you jump to your slices with an orange banner. Hold the sound you want and it drops onto that pad, then brings you back. The banner's buttons jump between Chopper, Play, Piano, Parts, Groups and the slice bin.
  • Piano and Play layers work the same way: tap 🎯 Load sound on the Piano, or hold LEAD / CHORDS / BASS in Play, then hold any sound.
  • ⏺ Record in the top bar works on every tab. Play live anywhere, press ⏹, then save the take as WAV or MP3 or send it to the slice bin to chop it again.
  • 🧠 Beat Brain reads your kits and writes a full song or one section. 🎁 Build packs drafts four curated kits from all your sounds: audition them, load one into a bank to edit, save it back, and export it as a zip.

Flip a sample into your own beat

  1. Cut the song in Chopper. ½× / 2× fixes the tempo if it's off.
  2. Here, fill a bank with 🔗 Smart chains, or tap ★ Sweet spots in the bin and load the ones you like.
  3. Pick P1, press ● REC and play a chop line. Chops cut each other off (cut group 3) like an MPC.
  4. 🧠 Smart Beat adds drums from your kit underneath. Press it again for another variation.
  5. Lay out patterns in the Song row, then ⬇ WAV, MIDI or the kit zip.

DJ the track

  1. Fill a bank with 🎧 Hot cues, then ⛶ Full screen.
  2. Tap cues to jump around the track. While the beat is running they wait for the next beat, so they always land in time.
  3. Use 🔁 Smart loops (tap on/off) to hold a section, then ride Roll, Echo, Gate, Filter and the Kill buttons. Finish with Backspin or Tape stop.
  4. ⏺ PRINT the whole set as you play it.

Make new instruments

  1. Find a ★ tonal slice. In ✏️ Edit, set Gate, trim the attack and tune it.
  2. Press L for Keys mode: the 16 keys play it in the song's key. Chord stacks triads.
  3. Record a melody, or PRINT a riff and chop the print again: resampling makes sounds the original never had.

The sweet-spot rules the app uses

  • Strong start: a chop should begin right on a hit, not just before or after it.
  • On the grid: starts that land on the 1, on a beat or on a 16th rank higher.
  • Clean tail: endings that fade before the next hit don't click and sit better when chained.
  • Musical lengths: chains add up to ½, 1, 2 beats or 1 bar. Loops are 1, 2 or 4 bars that repeat what comes after them.
  • Clear pitch: tonal slices with a confident pitch become instruments.
  • Phrasing: Smart Beat writes call-and-response (bar A, bar B, A again, then a change), leaves room for the snare, rolls hats into the next bar, and keeps chops off the kick's back.
  • Section changes: hot cues go where the energy or the number of hits jumps, nudged toward 4- and 8-bar boundaries.
